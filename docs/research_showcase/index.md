# Research Showcase

BRIN High Performance Computing empowers researchers across diverse scientific domains to tackle computationally demanding challenges. This page highlights selected scientific achievements, published papers, and research breakthroughs made possible by our computing facilities.

## 3. Submit Your Research Publication

Have you published a paper, thesis, or report that used BRIN HPC resources? We would love to feature your work!

Please send your citation details to `hpc-support@brin.go.id` with:

1. Publication title and author list.
2. Journal / Conference name, year, and DOI link.
3. A short summary (2–3 sentences) describing the role of HPC in your findings.
4. An optional high-resolution scientific visualization or plot for our highlight banner.
